§ Bill Companion

Most bills change existing law. Bill Companion automatically shows you the existing law.

Bills are parsed entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no server-side application — the U.S. Code is served as static files and the CFR is fetched from eCFR directly.
